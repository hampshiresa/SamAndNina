Travel Planning

Guests should fly into Manila (MNL), then take a domestic flight to Caticlan Airport (Boracay).

Time difference: Please be mindful of the time change when planning international connections and arrival times.

Important: Flights from Manila to Caticlan typically have their last departure around 3:30 PM. Depending on your arrival time in Manila, you may need to stay overnight before continuing to Boracay the next day.

If you are staying at The Lind: airport transfers will be arranged directly by the hotel from Caticlan to the resort.

Things to Do in Boracay

If you’re making a trip out of it (highly recommended), here are some fun ideas.

Island hopping / boat tours

Classic Boracay day — snorkeling stops + beaches + lunch options.

Sunset sail (Paraw)

Iconic sunset ride on a traditional sailboat. Great for photos.

Snorkel / scuba

Easy add-on if you like water activities — ask your hotel for trusted operators.

White Beach stroll

Sunset walk + shops + casual bars. Perfect “do nothing” afternoon.

Puka Shell Beach

A calmer beach vibe and a nice change of pace from the busiest areas.

Spa / massage day

Recover from travel. Great pre-wedding reset.

Nightlife

Beachfront lounges, live music, and nightlife spots (keep it fun + safe).

D’Mall + food crawl

Easy shopping + lots of dining options for groups.

Relax

Honestly… the best plan is beach time, good food, and no schedule.
